2. Change business name, phone, email, address, hours, social links, services, programs, testimonials and FAQs.
3. Put real images in assets/images/.
4. Change the image paths inside site-config.js.
5. For a real domain, replace example.com in canonical URLs and sitemap.xml/robots.txt.
